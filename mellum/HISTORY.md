# Historical preservation

User authorized branch commits and publication on 2026-11-06. Existing ancestors are unchanged.
All new commits are honest retrospective commits using current dates, not backdated original development events.
Stage manifests identify exact source paths and SHA256. Available abandoned alternatives and patches
are preserved under history/snapshots; reports explain measured results, invalid references and failures.
No claim is made that missing intermediate states or every edit can be recovered.
Large original evidence remains under /workspace/results; the two branches contain source and compact evidence.
No squash, rebase, amend, force push, or upstream publication is part of this operation.
